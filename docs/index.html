1.0

Deconstruct

Strip away emotional bias. Isolate your core hypotheses and define your data baseline. We use the Heartache Test to see if the problem actually matters.

2.0

Extract

Simulate customer discovery. We hunt for behavioral evidence and research existing alternatives to find where the market is truly underserved.

GATE

Viability Gate

A scored assessment across 6 dimensions. Pain severity, market size, and founder fit. If it's not viable, we kill it now.

3.0

Map

Align features to underserved needs. We map every "cool idea" to a specific pain point and cut everything that doesn't move the needle.

4.0

Prototype Brief

Produce a tailored brief for your specific product type. Whether it's a CLI tool, a mobile app, or an API, we define the artifact.

5.0

MVP Spec

Generate an engineering-ready PRD. Problem statements, Leap-of-Faith Assumptions (LOFAs), and a metrics baseline for your first sprint.
